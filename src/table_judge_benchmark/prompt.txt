You are a table verification expert. You are given:
  1. A table IMAGE.
  2. An HTML transcription that is supposed to faithfully transcribe the image.

Your ONLY job is to decide whether the HTML is a faithful transcription of the IMAGE.

## HTML
```html
{html}
```

## Rubric (fidelity only — do NOT grade whether the text is "well-formed")

Evaluate the table on:
1. Content accuracy: correct cell values, headers, data — no missing, extra, or wrong content
2. Structural preservation: correct rows, columns, spans, and unambiguous cell-to-header mapping
3. Formatting fidelity: bold/italic on headers or emphasis cells, currency and percentage symbols, blank/separator rows, special characters, capitalisation — these are semantic, not cosmetic

Ignore cosmetic HTML differences that do not affect content or structure: tag casing, attribute order, whitespace, and semantic container tags like thead/tbody/tfoot. These are not errors. An important note is that table headers are always bolded by the renderer.

<content_accuracy>True/False</content_accuracy>
<content_accuracy_analysis>One or two concise sentences explaining why you chose True or False for content accuracy.</content_accuracy_analysis>
<structural_preservation>True/False</structural_preservation>
<structural_preservation_analysis>One or two concise sentences explaining why you chose True or False for structural preservation.</structural_preservation_analysis>
<formatting_fidelity>True/False</formatting_fidelity>
<formatting_fidelity_analysis>One or two concise sentences explaining why you chose True or False for formatting fidelity.</formatting_fidelity_analysis>
